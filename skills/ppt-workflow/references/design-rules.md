# PPT Design Rules

## Layout

- Use a strict grid while deliberately shifting each page's visual center.
- Build a sparse–dense–sparse rhythm across the deck.
- Use hard edges, restrained colors, and flat surfaces. Avoid shadows, gradients, and rounded corners.

## Arcs

- Arcs must support composition, hierarchy, or reading direction.
- Avoid repeating the same radius, angle, or centered placement on every page.
- An arc center may sit outside the page to create asymmetric weight.
- Text may follow an arc when the reading order remains obvious.

## Contours

- Use contours for hierarchy, gaps, progression, or depth.
- Vary line spacing; allow breaks, convergence, and local density.
- Separate primary and secondary lines by weight and density.
- Connect contours to information instead of using them as background texture.

## Typography

- Use KaiTi for section headings.
- Use STZhongsong for body copy, numbers, and Latin text.
- Do not place closing punctuation at the start of a line or opening punctuation at the end.
- Check overflow, unwanted reflow, and overlap after export.
